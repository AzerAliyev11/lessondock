Introduction

LessonDock is a scheduling application designed for private tutors and teachers. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the information you enter into LessonDock under your control.

1. Information You Enter

LessonDock allows you to organize information related to your teaching activities, which may include:

  • Student names and lesson details
  • Class dates, times, and schedules
  • Payment amounts and due dates
  • Other information you choose to enter

This information is used to provide the app's scheduling and organizational features.

2. Data Storage

Information entered into LessonDock is stored locally on your device.

The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores your student information or lesson schedules.

3. Notifications

With your permission, LessonDock may send local notifications about upcoming lessons and payment due dates.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings.

4. Analytics and Advertising

LessonDock does not use third-party advertising services or analytics tools to track your activity.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Information Sharing

The developer does not share student records or lesson schedules with third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete it through the app, where supported, or remove the application and its locally stored data.

Device backup services may retain copies according to their own settings and policies.

7. Student Information

LessonDock is intended for use by teachers and tutors. Users are responsible for entering and managing student information appropriately and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.
